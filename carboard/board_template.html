How listings are matched

Prices are as advertised, before tax and licensing. When the same car is posted on more than one site it's shown once at its lowest price, with a link per site (AT, CG, KJ, CP; DLR is the dealer's own website). NEW means first seen in the last 24 hours; ▼/▲ show a price change in the last 7 days; ? marks a likely match whose sunroof only shows up in a feature checklist or description, so check the ad. A listing leaves the board once 3 complete scans of its site in a row no longer show it.
